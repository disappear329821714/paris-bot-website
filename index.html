Profiles · Connections · Listening

A little context for your community.

Look up Discord and Roblox profiles, resolve existing Bloxlink connections, and explore public Last.fm listening information.

Account lookups

Find public profile details and account identifiers. Bloxlink results depend on an existing connection and the server's integration; they are not proof of identity.

Listening information

See a Last.fm profile, recent tracks, current listening status and top artists, albums or tracks using a username.

Temporary channel history

Recent deleted messages, edits and removed reactions may be captured in accessible server text channels. The default history expires after 30 minutes. Read the privacy notice before using this feature.

Understand the data

Profile and listening results are generally posted in the channel where a command is used. Snipe replies are private to the requester, subject to channel permissions. Temporary memory does not mean no data processing.
